A Letter For You

❦ ❦ ❦ ❦
six months · september 7

My Love,

Six months with you, and somehow, “six months” feels too small for everything you mean to me.

There is something so quietly beautiful about having you in my life. You have become a part of my days in the most effortless way — not something I ever had to force, but something that simply found its place in my heart and stayed there.

I love you in the softest ways. In the way I think of you when I see something you'd like. In the way your name can make an ordinary moment feel a little warmer. In the way I can be completely myself around you and still feel understood. And maybe that's what I love most — you never had to be anything more than yourself for me to fall for you this deeply.

If I could put what you mean to me into one feeling, it would be the feeling of home — not a place, but a person. A person whose presence makes the world feel a little gentler, whose voice feels familiar, and whose existence alone can make a day feel better.

I don't think love is always supposed to be loud. Sometimes it's simply knowing that there's one person you want to tell everything to. One person whose happiness genuinely matters to you. One person you look at and quietly think, “I'm really lucky that it's you.”

And that's exactly how I feel about you.

I hope you always know how deeply you are loved, how beautifully you are appreciated, and how special your place in my heart is. Not because of anything you do, not because you have to be perfect, but simply because you are you.

Six months, my love. And if there's one thing I know for sure, it's that I would still choose you in every version of this story.

Happy six months. ❤️

You are, and will always be, one of the most beautiful parts of my life.

I love you. More quietly than words can explain, and more deeply than words ever could.

yours, always ♡
